# Names, marks and keys

The Apache License 2.0 that covers this repository grants rights in its code and documents. As its
section 6 says, it grants none to use the licensor's names or marks. In particular it grants no rights to:

- the name "Sapling" as the name of this project and of sapling.cash, and the Sapling logo. (Sapling is
  also the name of a Zcash protocol and shielded pool; that name is Zcash's and is not claimed here.)
- the domain sapling.cash;
- Sapling's stamp issuer keys and request keys, and the fee account and addresses in
  `params/mainnet.json`, which identify Sapling's stamps.

Forks and other deployments are welcome under the License. They must:

- use their own name, and not present themselves as Sapling or as endorsed by it;
- use their own issuer and request keys and their own parameter file;
- not present their stamps as Sapling stamps: a Sapling stamp is one that `params/mainnet.json` in this
  repository recognises.

Saying that a deployment implements the Sapling stamps specification is fine.
